# Product rules
1. The home screen answers one question: what do I do today?
2. Plain language. No fitness jargon.
3. A check-in takes three taps.
4. Show food as portions he can picture.
5. Every plan has one line saying why.
6. Calories and protein come from `calculator.ts`. The AI never invents them.
7. Pain or injury: suggest a doctor or trainer. This is not medical advice.
